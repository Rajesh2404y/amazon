BLOCKING EXPERIMENT COMPARISON: BASELINE VS. OPTION A (5,000 S1 ENTITIES)

1. ARCHITECTURAL COMPARISON
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Approach 1: Baseline (Post-Union Global Truncation)
  - Method: Combined hits from all blocking views into a single list, then 
    truncated with a hard cap (top 25-45).
  - Flaw: High-frequency character 3-gram hits flooded the candidate pool, 
    displacing high-precision exact name and address matches.

Approach 2: Option A (Independent Per-View Quotas with Pure Mathematical Union)
  - Name Quota:    Top-25 candidates
  - Address Quota: Top-15 candidates
  - Char Quota:    Top-10 candidates
  - Combination:   Pure set union (no post-union global cap)
  - Mathematical Guarantee: Strict monotonicity and bounded worst-case size 
    (<= 25 + 15 + 10 = 50 candidates per S1 entity).
